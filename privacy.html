Who is responsible?

Dibnib is a daily knowledge app developed and operated by Giordano Menegazzi, the controller responsible for the personal data described here. For privacy questions and requests, contact dibnibApp@outlook.com.

The essentials

You can read Dibnib without creating a named account. Dibnib does not contain advertising or third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, sell your personal data, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Some information is still needed to deliver content, remember interactions and keep the service secure.

Information processed by Dibnib

  • Content requests: the requested content, language and time zone are sent to our backend so that we can return the appropriate Nibs and daily edition. A time zone is not your precise location.
  • Installation identity: when an interaction needs it, the app creates a random installation identifier and authentication token. The backend stores the identifier, a hash of the token, and creation and last-active timestamps. This is a pseudonymous identifier, not your name or email address.
  • Interactions: likes, completed share events and content reports are associated with that installation identifier and the relevant Nib. Share events include a random event ID to avoid counting retries twice. We do not receive the recipients or private messages from the iOS share sheet. Reports include the selected reason and any comment you choose to submit where supported. Aggregate interaction counts may be shown in the app.
  • Technical information: servers receive your IP address and ordinary network-request information. Web-server logs can include request time, requested URL, response status, user agent and referrer. We also keep aggregate daily request and error counts.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments you send.

Information kept on your device

The app stores settings, preferences, downloaded content and interaction state on your device. Installation credentials and pending interaction records are stored in the iOS Keychain. Keychain records may survive deleting and reinstalling the app. Device backups are governed by your Apple settings; the installation credentials use a device-only Keychain protection class.

Purchases and Pro access

Apple processes App Store payments and subscriptions. The app uses StoreKit to read verified purchase and subscription status to unlock Pro features. We do not receive your card details. If complimentary Pro access is granted, the backend stores the installation identifier and grant expiry. Apple processes purchase information under its Privacy Policy.

Why we process information

We process content requests, installation credentials, interactions and entitlement information to provide the features you use (performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We process security logs and service statistics to protect the service, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and improve reliability, and support correspondence to answer questions (legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f)). Where applicable, we process information to meet a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). Optional interaction features are not required simply to read content. We do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Service providers and external links

Our backend hosting and infrastructure providers process the network and service data needed to operate Dibnib. Support email is handled through Microsoft Outlook. These legal pages are hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives visitor network information; see GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Microsoft’s Privacy Statement. Access is limited to what is needed for the relevant service. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect legal rights. Source links and other external websites have their own privacy practices. Following a source link sends ordinary browser request information to that website.

International processing

Apple, Microsoft, GitHub and their service providers may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Where GDPR requires safeguards for a transfer we arrange, the transfer must be covered by an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. You can contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data. The linked provider notices explain their own international processing.

How long information is kept

Installation records and associated interactions are retained while needed to provide those features, unless you request deletion or the data is no longer necessary. Likes can be removed by unliking a Nib. Server access and error logs use daily rotation with up to 14 rotated files plus the active file; skipped or empty rotations mean this is not a guaranteed 14-day deletion deadline. Support correspondence is kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and handle any related legal obligations or disputes. Deleted data may remain temporarily in restricted backups until those recovery copies are replaced or expire. Aggregate statistics that no longer identify an installation may be retained.

Your choices and rights

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact dibnibApp@outlook.com to exercise these rights, including deletion of backend installation data. Because we do not have a named account for you, we may need your help to securely identify the relevant installation; never email your authentication token. Simply uninstalling the app does not delete backend records or cancel an Apple subscription. We normally respond to GDPR requests within one month, subject to the extensions permitted by law. You may complain to your local data protection authority; in the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Children and security

Dibnib is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information beyond the service data described above. If you believe a child has provided personal information that should be removed, contact dibnibApp@outlook.com. We use HTTPS, hashed backend tokens and device Keychain storage to protect information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its processing changes. The date at the top identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through the app or another appropriate notice.
